The move-tab shortcuts need to know whether the tab group next to the active tab is collapsed. chrome.tabs reports which group a tab belongs to but not whether that group is collapsed, so the extension calls chrome.tabGroups.query with collapsed: true for the current window and uses the result to decide between adding the tab to the group and moving it past the collapsed group in a single step. The extension never creates, renames, recolors, or closes a group, and it reads nothing about a group beyond its id and collapsed state.
